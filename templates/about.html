OUR IDEA

Travel should feel easy.

DriveEase is a learning project that demonstrates how a car rental website can bring vehicle browsing, booking forms and simple reservation records together.

Browse a small sample fleet, enter your journey details and receive an estimated rental total. The backend stores demo bookings in a local SQLite database.
